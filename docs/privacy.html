Privacy Policy — ttv2yt

Last updated: October 11, 2026.

What data the app accesses

Via Google OAuth the app requests a single scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, used only to upload video files to the operator's own YouTube channel as private videos. The app also receives the basic Google account identifier needed to complete OAuth.

How data is used

  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the operator's own computer (token.json) and never transmitted anywhere except to Google's OAuth and YouTube API endpoints.
  • Uploaded videos go only to the operator's own channel, as private videos.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data retention and deletion

Tokens persist until revoked. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the local token.json file. Clips stored locally can be deleted from the data/clips folder.
